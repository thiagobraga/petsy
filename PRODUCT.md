# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pet owners managing their companion animals' health and wellbeing. They use the app daily to track medicines, appointments, and memories. Context: home, on-the-go, mobile-first. The job to be done: simple, reassuring tracking that feels supportive and reduces anxiety about pet care.

## Product Purpose

Petsy is a calm, gentle pet care management companion that helps owners track medicines, appointments, diary notes, and memories without feeling clinical or overwhelming. Success = pet owners feel more confident and connected to their pets' health journey.

## Brand Personality

Calm, supportive, delicate, warm. Like a trusted veterinary nurse who listens and reassures, not a corporate tool. Personality: gentle, approachable, trustworthy, slightly playful but never chaotic.

## Anti-references

- Medical/clinical design (too sterile, intimidating)
- Bubbly/cartoonish (undermines trust in health tracking)
- Flat, corporate SaaS (boring, impersonal)
- Maximalist (overwhelming pet owners with visual noise)

## Design Principles

1. **Calm over clever** — Every interaction reduces anxiety, not adds it. Clarity beats delight.
2. **Warmth through subtlety** — Softness, gentle curves, and muted richness, not brightness or punch.
3. **Delicate & refined** — Less is more. Remove visual weight, reduce borders, let whitespace breathe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body text minimum)
- Placeholder text meets contrast minimums
- Reduced motion respected (no auto-play, respectful animations)
- Touch targets 48px+ (mobile-first iOS feel)
